# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers, technical founders, and autonomous coding agents integrating an OpenAI-compatible inference endpoint. They arrive with an existing harness such as OpenClaw, OpenCode, Hermes-style runners, or a standard SDK and need to authenticate, understand the single-model contract, and make a reliable first agentic call quickly.

## Product Purpose

Vantis Cards turns a verified public identity into metered AI infrastructure. The product scores a developer, grants a non-transferable inference balance, issues scoped API keys, serves DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 through an OpenAI-compatible rail, and records the real per-call cost as a virtual off-chain $VANTIS burn. Success means the product is honest, operationally legible, agent-compatible, and simple to integrate without operator help.

## Brand Personality

Precise, infrastructural, and candid. The product should feel like a serious developer rail with a distinct Vantis signal, not a speculative token campaign or a generic AI dashboard.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a neon crypto casino, a glassmorphic AI template, a fictional multi-model router, or a marketing page that hides operational constraints. Never imply that virtual credits are transferable tokens, that the virtual burn is on-chain, or that zero data retention is active when it is not verifiably honored.

## Design Principles

1. Put the first successful API call within one screen of arrival.
2. State the exact model, price, limits, and settlement behavior without euphemism.
3. Treat agentic streaming and tool calls as first-class workflows, not edge cases.
4. Keep provider topology private while making Vantis-level guarantees observable.
5. Prefer familiar developer affordances: stable navigation, copyable code, precise errors, and readable reference tables.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve keyboard focus, support reduced motion, keep body copy within readable line lengths, provide at least 32px mobile targets, and prevent horizontal overflow at 390px. Documentation must remain usable without JavaScript except for progressive features such as search and copy buttons.
